Try it in 60 seconds

Put the screen on your laptop. Vote from your phone.

  1. Open the big screen on a laptop or TV and press F for full screen.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone camera and answer the three questions.
  3. Watch the results move. Use → / ← or a clicker to flip between questions.
  4. Take the operator seat to hide results, add company tags or start a 90-second timer.

Built for the room

Details that matter on the day.

Truly anonymous

A random device ID, no names, no emails, no IP addresses in the data. Only aggregates are public.

Offline-tolerant

Lost signal? Answers are saved on the phone and retried automatically after 2, 4 and 8 seconds.

No double counting

Go back and change an answer — it is updated, never duplicated. Reopen the link and pick up where you left off.

Calm animations

Bars grow smoothly and reorder at most every four seconds, so the audience can actually read them.

Operator in control

Test and live sessions, reset with confirmation, CSV exports and a PNG of every results page.

Light as air

The phone page loads in a fraction of a second on 4G. No frameworks, no trackers, no pop-ups.
